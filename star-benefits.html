STAR Team
Membership Benefits Guide

Official English edition • January 2026

Matt Gall, Ph.D.
Chief Strategy Officer, STAR Team

The STAR Team Membership Benefits System is built around long-term collaboration, genuine trading activity, and regulatory compliance. All rewards and privileges are granted on the basis of verified accounts, consistent participation, and meaningful contribution to the team’s development.

Communication Channels

STAR – Centre de Documentation Officielle

Official documentation hub for all STAR Team members. Contains institutional rules, membership benefits, updates, onboarding materials, and compliance guidelines.

STAR - Groupe de discussion sur les actions

Primary channel for signal codes, daily learning materials, major announcements, member benefits, and the weekly Thursday New Member Training (open to all members).

Groupe de formation des agents VIP STAR

Exclusive group for VIP agents. Hosts weekly Friday meetings, management training, and agent-level coordination.

STAR Official Account & Four Departments

STAR Discipline & Compliance Committee (DCC)

Highest authority for rule-making and disciplinary oversight.

  • • Formulates and updates trading discipline and conduct rules
  • • Investigates violations and issues final sanctions
  • • Holds compliance veto power over all departments

Risk Control & Position Management Center (RCPM)

Central risk management hub ensuring execution stability.

  • • Monitors position structure and risk exposure in real time
  • • Manages drawdown limits and concentration risk
  • • Intervenes on abnormal trading activity

Investment Research & Operations Office (IROO)

Research center and official information release window.

  • • Conducts long-term market research
  • • Publishes official operational notices
  • • Manages internal and external communications

Financial Governance & Audit Center (FGAC)

Financial oversight and audit management center.

  • • Reviews all fund applications and disbursements
  • • Ensures transparency and auditability of fund flows
  • • Works with DCC on financial audits

Core Operating Principles

• Research and execution are separated
• Compliance takes precedence over returns
• All financial processes are traceable and auditable
• Rules are uniformly interpreted and applied
• Major decisions are released through official channels

Important Notice

All operational announcements published by IROO are for informational purposes only. They do not constitute investment advice, profit guarantees, or trading recommendations.
