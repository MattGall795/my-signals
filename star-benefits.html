Membership Rights, Rewards, Structure, and Governance Framework

This document sets out the STAR Team membership rights system, including referral rewards, VIP agent ACM revenue sharing, fixed bonuses, signal rewards, collective team signals, copy-trading limits, VIP growth pathways, official learning channels, and the four-department governance mechanism.

Important: All rewards and benefits are based on compliance, real accounts, continuous participation, and team contribution. This system emphasizes stability, structure, and long-term value, rather than short-term stimulation.

1. General Policy Principles

The STAR Team membership rights system is built around the following areas:

  • Referral rewards
  • VIP agent ACM revenue sharing
  • Monthly fixed bonuses for VIP agents
  • STAR signal rewards
  • STAR collective signal rewards
  • Copy-trading limits and trading channel benefits
  • VIP growth and responsibility system
  • Official learning documents and meeting system
  • Four-department collaborative governance mechanism

3. STAR Official Account Service Channel

The STAR Official Account centrally distributes information from the four major departments.

1) STAR Discipline & Compliance Committee (DCC)

  • Formulates and updates STAR trading discipline and conduct standards
  • Monitors violations, abnormal operations, and rule circumvention
  • Has final authority over violation handling and account removal
  • Has compliance review and veto authority over all departments

2) STAR Risk Control & Position Management Center (RPMC)

  • Monitors position structure, risk exposure, and fund usage in real time
  • Manages drawdown, concentration risk, and systemic risk
  • Intervenes in abnormal positions, overtrading, and risk imbalance
  • Provides the risk framework for execution and research

3) STAR Investment Research & Operations Office (IROO)

  • Conducts long-term stock market research and strategy building
  • Produces logical, executable, and reviewable research conclusions
  • Publishes official operational announcements and research notes
  • Manages communication pacing and professional expression

4) STAR Financial Governance & Audit Center (FGAC)

  • Reviews compliance of fund requests, disbursements, and usage
  • Supervises fund flow records and traceability
  • Works with DCC on financial audit matters
  • Ensures transparency, traceability, and auditability

4. Operating Principles of the STAR Team

  • Research and execution are separated
  • Risk control and expansion are both emphasized
  • Compliance takes priority over profit expression
  • Financial flows must be traceable and auditable
  • Rules are interpreted and executed uniformly
  • Major decisions are issued through official channels
  • All members must follow discipline and cooperation standards

5. Disclaimer on STAR Investment Department Operational Announcements

Operational announcements published by the STAR Investment Research & Operations Office are based on long-term stock research and risk assessment mechanisms. They are stage-based research and operational explanations intended to help members understand market conditions and system rhythm.

They do not constitute:

  • Profit guarantees
  • Investment commitments
  • Specific stock buy/sell recommendations
  • Performance guarantees

14. Unified Compliance and Risk Management Requirements

  • All accounts must be real accounts.
  • One person using multiple accounts is prohibited.
  • Money laundering and abnormal fund behavior are prohibited.
  • Fake invitations, fake deposits, fake trades, and reward abuse are prohibited.
  • All rewards must be based on compliant accounts, qualified balances, and real participation.
  • Members must cooperate with compliance, risk, and operations reviews.
  • Violators may lose rewards, have profits recovered, be terminated, or lose agent status.

15. Core Principles of the System

  • Long-term cooperation first
  • Real trading first

  • Continuous participation first
  • Team contribution first

16. Conclusion

The STAR Team membership rights system is a comprehensive framework built around referral rewards, fixed agent rewards, protective benefits, signal rewards, collective team signals, copy-trading limits, and VIP growth pathways.

Through Bonchat groups, the Official Account service channel, and the four-department governance mechanism, STAR has created a complete system for learning, research, risk control, compliance, finance, and communication.

The core goal of this policy is to promote long-term, stable, compliant, and sustainable development through clear level standards, transparent reward mechanisms, and disciplined agent responsibilities.
