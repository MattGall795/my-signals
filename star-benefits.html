Member Rights & Benefits

STAR Team Member Rights
and Benefits Regulations

Effective from 2026

Article 1 – General Provisions

The STAR Team Member Rights and Benefits System is designed to provide learning support, tool access, team collaboration services, and resource allocation based on each member’s level of participation.

This regulation applies to members who have completed basic registration, identity verification, and platform rule learning.

The system emphasizes the following principles:

  • Compliant participation
  • Real accounts
  • Long-term collaboration
  • Stable development
  • Transparent rules

All rights and benefits are allocated and adjusted according to platform rules, account status, team collaboration performance, and actual level of participation.

Article 2 – Member Levels and Basic Support

1. Ordinary Member

Applicable to newly registered members.

  • • Basic learning materials
  • • Beginner operation guides
  • • Official announcements and rule explanations
  • • Basic signal reference access
2. Advanced Member

Applicable to members who have completed account configuration, learning tasks, or team collaboration records.

  • • More comprehensive signal reference access
  • • Higher-frequency strategy explanations
  • • Internal learning materials
  • • Priority access to training and Q&A sessions
3. VIP Member

Applicable to members who meet platform requirements and maintain active and compliant status.

  • • Higher-level signal reference services
  • • More complete team collaboration support
  • • Dedicated communication channels
  • • Priority participation in team activities and project briefings

Article 3 – Referral and Collaboration Incentives

To encourage members to actively participate in platform development and support new members, the STAR Team has established a collaboration-based incentive mechanism.

Referral Rules:

  • Account balance ≤ 2000: Each successful referral of one valid new member grants 500 points of allocation reference support.
  • Account balance between 2000 and 4000: Each successful referral of one valid new member grants 800 points of allocation reference support.

These rules serve only as reference for resource allocation and are subject to dynamic adjustment.

Article 4 – Team Collaboration Benefits

Members who actively participate in STAR Team collaboration, support the promotion of the ACM platform, and contribute to team building will receive priority access to:

• Higher-level signal reference permissions
• Priority channel resource allocation
• More complete training materials
• Higher-frequency team communication support

Article 5 – Agent-Related Benefits

1. Agent Status

An agent is a member approved by the STAR Team who assumes responsibility for team communication, order maintenance, information transmission, and assisting new members.

2. Agent Benefits

  • • Team management tool permissions
  • • Dedicated agent communication support
  • • Priority participation in collaborative tasks
  • • Internal training and operational briefings

3. Agent Classification

Agent levels are evaluated comprehensively based on team size, number of collaborators, activity level, and actual contribution.

Article 6 – Signal Service Benefits

1. Basic Signal Support — Members can access signal reference content corresponding to their account level.

2. Permanent Signal Support — Members who reach higher levels and maintain compliant status may obtain long-term higher-level signal support.

3. Additional Signal Support — Additional signals may be granted when members achieve effective collaboration or meet team stage goals. The quantity is dynamically determined by the system.

Article 7 – Team Collective Benefits

When a team reaches a certain scale and achieves phased goals, all members may receive additional collective rewards, such as more signal opportunities, higher-level communication permissions, and access to team-exclusive activities.

Article 8 – Account and Compliance Requirements

  • • Use real accounts and do not create multiple accounts
  • • Do not engage in money laundering, fraud, or false registration
  • • Do not falsify referral relationships or team data
  • • Do not intentionally disrupt team order
  • • Account balance and participation status must meet platform requirements

Article 9 – Rights Adjustment Mechanism

If an account has not formed effective collaboration with the team for more than 3 months and has no promotion records or team-building records, the system will automatically remove the account from the current tracking permission pool and suspend part of its benefits.

Article 10 – Final Provisions

This regulation does not pursue short-term stimulation, does not promise fixed returns, and does not guarantee any investment returns. All benefits are granted based on real participation, continuous collaboration, and compliant status.
