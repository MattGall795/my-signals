1. General Policy Overview

The STAR Team Member Benefits System is built around the following core dimensions:

  • Referral rewards
  • VIP agent ACM profit-sharing income
  • Monthly fixed bonuses for VIP agents
  • STAR signal rewards
  • STAR team collective signal rewards
  • Copy-trading quota and trading channel benefits
  • VIP growth and responsibility structure
  • Official learning documents and meeting system
  • Four-department collaborative governance mechanism

The purpose of this policy is to encourage long-term cooperation, real trading, and orderly development. All rewards and benefits are based on compliance, real accounts, continuous participation, and team contribution. The system does not pursue short-term excitement, but rather stability, structure, and long-term value.

3. STAR Official Account Service Overview

The STAR Official Account centrally distributes service information from the four major departments. It is the important window for official policies, notices, audits, risk control, and research information.

3.1 STAR Discipline & Compliance Committee (DCC)

Positioning: The highest rule-making and discipline supervision body in the STAR system.

Core responsibilities:

  • Formulate, interpret, and continuously update STAR stock trading discipline and behavioral standards
  • Supervise and handle rule violations, abnormal operations, and bypass behaviors
  • Hold final decision power over violating accounts, teams, and individuals, including removal decisions
  • Have compliance review and veto authority over all departments and operations

3.2 STAR Risk Control & Position Management Center (RPMC)

Positioning: The central risk boundary management hub of the system.

Core responsibilities:

  • Monitor account positions, risk exposure, and capital usage in real time
  • Manage maximum drawdown, concentration, and potential systemic risks
  • Intervene in abnormal positions, overtrading, and risk imbalance situations
  • Provide clear risk constraints for research and execution systems

3.3 STAR Investment Research & Operations Office (IROO)

Positioning: The research center and official information release window.

Core responsibilities:

  • Conduct long-term stock market research and strategy framework building
  • Produce logically complete, executable, and reviewable research conclusions
  • Unified release of official operation notices and research explanations
  • Manage information pacing and professional communication

3.4 STAR Financial Governance & Audit Center (FGAC)

Positioning: The capital flow and audit management hub.

Core responsibilities:

  • Review compliance of fund applications, disbursements, and usage
  • Supervise the completeness and reasonableness of fund flows and records
  • Support disciplinary and compliance audit work
  • Ensure the fund system is transparent, traceable, and auditable

4. Team Operating Principles

STAR Team operates with an institutional structure where research, risk control, compliance, and finance balance one another. Responsibilities are clearly separated, and the system does not rely on individual judgment. Instead, it is driven by rules, process, and long-term research.

Operating principles include:

  • Separation of research and execution
  • Risk control and expansion in balance
  • Compliance comes before profit expression
  • Financial processes must be traceable and auditable
  • Unified interpretation and unified enforcement of rules
  • Important decisions are announced through official mechanisms
  • All members must comply with discipline and cooperation standards

5. Notes on STAR Investment Operations Announcements

The operation announcements issued by the STAR Investment Research & Operations Office are based on long-term stock research and risk assessment mechanisms. They are phased operational and research explanations.

The purpose is to help members understand market conditions and system rhythm. They do not constitute any form of:

  • Profit guarantee
  • Investment commitment
  • Individual stock buy/sell recommendation
  • Outcome guarantee

Members should consider their own situation and interpret all official explanations rationally.

9. STAR Agent Responsibilities

STAR agents should actively participate in team building, support member communication, coordination, and daily assistance, and help maintain normal team order.

Agents must comply with STAR discipline, compliance, and behavior standards. They may not engage in anything that harms the team’s reputation, breaks rules, or disrupts the stability of the system.

Agents must transmit information accurately and maintain order, ensuring that team members correctly understand official STAR announcements, rules, and policy descriptions. They must not distort or expand official statements without authorization.

Agents should maintain activity and stability, help new members become familiar with the system, and promote long-term cooperation and structured development.

Agents must cooperate with compliance, risk control, and operations requirements and accept necessary reviews and assessments. If they do not continue to fulfill their duties, their agent identity and related benefits may be adjusted or terminated according to the rules.

14. Unified Compliance and Risk Management Requirements

All members and agents must comply with the following requirements:

  • All accounts must be real accounts.
  • Multiple accounts by one person are prohibited.
  • Money laundering and abnormal fund behavior are prohibited.
  • False invitations, false deposits, false trades, or malicious reward farming are prohibited.
  • All rewards must be based on compliant accounts, qualified balances, and real participation.
  • Members must cooperate with STAR compliance review, risk review, and operations management requirements.
  • Violators may lose rewards, have earnings recovered, have cooperation terminated, or lose agent status.

The STAR Team Member Benefits System follows these principles:

  • Stability, structure, and long-term value are emphasized

16. Closing Statement

The STAR Team Member Benefits System is a comprehensive structure built around referral rewards, agent fixed rewards, basic benefits, signal rewards, team signals, copy-trading quotas, and the VIP growth system.

With Bonchat groups, the Official Account service channel, and the collaborative governance of the four departments, STAR Team has formed a complete system for learning, research, risk control, compliance, finance, and operational publishing.

This policy aims to promote long-term, stable, compliant, and sustainable development through clear tier standards, reward mechanisms, and agent responsibilities.
