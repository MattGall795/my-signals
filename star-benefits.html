Member Rights & Benefits Detailed Specification

I. General Provisions

The STAR Team rights system is established around the following core pillars:

  • Referral Rewards & STAR Signal Bonuses
  • VIP Agent ACM Profit Sharing & Fixed Monthly Incentives
  • Team Collective Signals & Copy-trading Quota Allocation
  • VIP Growth & Responsibilities Framework
  • Four-department Synergy Governance

This system encourages long-term collaboration, authentic trading, and compliant development. All rewards are based on Real Accounts and Consistent Participation.

III. STAR Official Account Governance

1. STAR Discipline & Compliance Committee (DCC)

The highest regulatory authority. Sets the rules, monitors violations, and holds the final power of account termination and team expulsion.

2. STAR Risk Control & Position Management Center (RCPM)

The risk boundary guardian. Monitors position structures, manages maximum drawdowns, and intercedes in cases of systematic risk or over-trading.

3. STAR Investment Research & Operations Office (IROO)

The intellectual hub. Formulates long-term strategy frameworks and issues official operational research announcements.

4. STAR Financial Governance & Audit Center (FGAC)

Financial oversight. Ensures all fund flows are transparent, auditable, and compliant with financial governance protocols.

IV. Core Operating Principles

The STAR Team adopts an institutionalized structure where research, risk control, compliance, and finance are mutually balanced. Decisions are driven by Rules and Long-term Research, not individual judgment.

V. STAR Investment Department Operational Announcements

Announcements are operational explanations based on market research. They do not constitute: Profit Guarantees, Investment Commitments, or Individual Buy/Sell Recommendations.
